TelcoPulse CRM is an enterprise customer retention cloud designed specifically for telecom operators. It integrates real-time churn propensity diagnostics with frontline customer success workflows, enabling retention managers to preemptively identify at-risk subscribers, understand their exact human pain points, and deploy targeted incentives before contract cancellation.

⚡

Customer 360 & Churn Risk Engine

Core Analytics

Calculates a live Customer Health Score (0–100) and Churn Probability based on billing amounts, contract commitment, and service history.

  • Instant predictive scoring with color-coded risk tiers
  • Includes realistic customer persona presets for quick testing
  • Calculates monthly and cumulative charges ratios
✨

AI Retention Copilot ('Aria')

Humanized Outreach

Acts as an intelligent retention co-pilot for account reps. Synthesizes risk factors into plain-English reasons, phone talking points, and tailored outreach drafts.

  • Generates tailored Email, Phone Call Scripts, and SMS
  • Recommends 1-click executable retention actions
  • Removes robotic buzzwords in favor of natural conversational empathy
👥

Subscribers CRM Directory

Account Management

Complete subscriber repository enriched with real customer names, contact info, assigned account managers, customer sentiment, and health scores.

  • Search across thousands of records by name, email, or account ID
  • Filter by contract structure and account status
  • One-click "Open Account" opens the Customer 360 workspace
🧬

Behavioral Customer Personas

Strategic Clustering

Groups subscribers into 4 strategic behavioral personas using unsupervised K-Means clustering across billing behavior, tenure, and digital service adoption.

  • Identifies High-Value Month-to-Month spenders vs. Stable Economy users
  • Provides average tenure, spend, and segment churn rate
  • Prescribes battle-tested retention playbooks per group
📁

Batch Health Audits

Bulk Operations

Enables retention teams to upload entire subscriber CSV exports to batch-score churn probabilities, cluster personas, and highlight priority churn candidates.

  • Drag-and-drop CSV processing with automatic schema alignment
  • Calculates aggregate batch churn rates and at-risk counts
  • Prepares prioritized outreach queues for call center teams
